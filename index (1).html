Spiritual self-deception rarely feels like rebellion. It usually feels like wisdom, busyness, privacy, humility, caution, or "I'm just not ready."

But if the result is delayed obedience, hidden sin, shallow Scripture engagement, weak repentance, little visible fruit, or no investment in helping others follow Jesus, the story deserves to be examined.

The goal of this assessment is not shame. The goal is truth that leads to freedom, obedience, and fruit.
